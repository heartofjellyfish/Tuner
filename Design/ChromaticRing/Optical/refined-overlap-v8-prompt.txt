Use case: ui-mockup
Create a sophisticated minimalist high fidelity iPhone UI concept board for Clean Tuner: three consistent tall full-screen Chromatic tuner interfaces side by side on an ivory canvas. Modern Teenage Engineering restraint, beautiful deeply saturated matte ultramarine blue background, warm white precise typography. NO bulky glass, thick bevels, luminous outlines on every cell or toy-like controls. Each screen simple top header "Chromatic", thin back chevron and settings gear, nine tiny live input vertical rounded bars under header. A large centered clean circular annular chromatic dial, with central large sharp A₂ in ivory.

This is an elegant pitch RANGE ALIGNMENT display inspired by the clear segmented chromatic wheel of TonalEnergy, with our own blue minimal aesthetic. Twelve evenly sized 30-degree annular note regions. The band thickness is moderate, about 32 pixels for radius 160 pixels. Unlit regions are almost flat blue-on-blue with tiny thin crisp gaps between each region: clearly readable boundaries, no strong outline frame around each segment. NO beveling, no embossed shading, no metallic 3D, no extruded wedges. Note names placed centered INSIDE the annular regions, sharp warm ivory, no labels outside circle. Clockwise A at top, A♯ at1clock, B at2, C at3, C♯ at4, D at5, D♯ at6, E at7, F at8, F♯ at9, G at10, G♯ at11. Fixed labels remain exactly same positions in all screens. A region has crisp fixed white boundary seams at 15 degrees either side of top. Other region separators much quieter.

A same-width moving light AREA overlays the annulus: a translucent pale ivory 30-degree curved annular band whose TWO radial edges remain sharply visible. Soft understated optical light appears ONLY around this active region, never around rest of dial. Fixed A target region boundaries and moving band boundaries are visible at the same radius. The active A label bold navy on illuminated area; other labels muted ivory. The light area has precise edges and quiet beautiful frosted luminosity but flat UI construction.

Left: moving light region displaced counterclockwise from A by about 7.5 degrees (25ct flat), clearly see uncovered sliver on right edge of fixed A region and light spilling into previous G♯ region on left. Center A₂. 
Middle: moving light region nearly matches A, offset counterclockwise just 2.4 degrees (8ct flat), tiny gaps at both sides visible. Center A₂.
Right: moving light band EXACTLY fills A region edge to edge. Active region simple luminous mint, its boundaries coincide perfectly with fixed boundaries. A label dark blue inside mint band. Center A₂ with small quiet mint circular success accent below center note, no smiley face. Restrained mint light bloom beyond active region only.

Overall restrained and editorial: blue ground, quiet unlit ring, one beautifully lit section, clean large central note. Generous whitespace, no footer information, no cents in UI, no sharp/flat instructions, no needles, no dots tracking pitch, no yellow/orange, no rainbow. Under each screen on presentation canvas, tiny captions "−25 ct", "−8 ct", "0 ct". The single meaningful distinction between screens is edge overlap and mint aligned state. Outstanding polished contemporary UI design.
